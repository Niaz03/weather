Who we are

About Weather Kite 🪁

Weather Kite is an independent weather dashboard built to make live conditions, short-range forecasts, and rain radar easy to read at a glance — for anyone from casual planners to hikers, farmers, and agronomists.

We don't run our own weather satellites or radar stations. Instead, we pull together live and historical observations from trusted open providers — Open-Meteo for current conditions, forecasts, and air quality, RainViewer for near-real-time precipitation radar, and NASA GIBS for true-color satellite cloud imagery — and present them in one clean, fast interface.

What Weather Kite offers

Live current conditions, an hourly forecast, a 16-day outlook, animated precipitation radar with a satellite cloud view, sunrise/sunset and moon phase, live air quality, a daily life index for everyday decisions, saved locations, switchable °C/°F and km/h/mph units, and rule-based field advisories tailored to hikers, farmers, and agricultural use.

A note on accuracy

Weather Kite is a demonstration product built on public data sources. While we aim for accuracy, no forecast is perfect — please don't rely on it for emergencies or safety-critical decisions. See our Terms & Conditions for details.
